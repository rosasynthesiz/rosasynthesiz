# Rosa Synthesiz

Sovereign AI on your own machine. We build our own models and tools for voice, audio and video in Indian languages, and they run on hardware you own.

## What we're building

- **Reactive voices** (in training): AI voices that talk with you the way a friend does. They react while you speak, laugh at your joke and go soft when you share bad news. Starting with Tamil, then more Indian languages.
- **Naavil**: offline dictation for Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada and English. Mac and Windows. Beta.
- **Speech stack**: ASR and TTS for Tamil and Tanglish, on-device on Apple silicon and consumer NVIDIA GPUs, with an evaluation harness for every model we test.
- **Media pipelines**: videos drawn and rendered from code (canvas, Blender, ffmpeg) with automated checks on timing, levels and sync.
- **Agent infrastructure**: small-model serving, routing and multi-agent workflows on a single workstation, with a human approving every action.

## Open source

- [flstudio-mcp](https://github.com/rosasynthesiz/flstudio-mcp): AI control of FL Studio through MCP. Mix inspection, gain staging, plug-ins, routing and MIDI; 67 tools.
- [logic-pro-mcp](https://github.com/rosasynthesiz/logic-pro-mcp): AI control of Logic Pro on macOS. Transport, tracks, mixer, plug-ins and bounce, with every result read back from Logic.
- [claude-animation-studio](https://github.com/rosasynthesiz/claude-animation-studio): a Claude Code skill that turns a script into a short animated video with local voice and a measured mix.
- [wifi-room-radar](https://github.com/rosasynthesiz/wifi-room-radar): an experiment in WiFi CSI room sensing (presence, motion, breathing estimates) with a built-in simulator.

All four are MIT licensed.

## How we work

Reproducible setup, pinned dependencies, measured results. We say what a tool can and cannot do. Open models we host on Hugging Face keep their original licences and credits; any change we make is stated on the model page, and every file is hash-checked.
